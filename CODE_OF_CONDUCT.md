# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a useful, welcoming place for people working on search evaluation, product tooling, and reviewer workflows.

Contributors are expected to participate with respect, curiosity, and care. Disagreement is fine; harassment, personal attacks, or exclusionary behavior are not.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful of different levels of experience and domain context.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions constructive and specific.
- Assume good intent, and make room for correction when something lands badly.
- Avoid sharing private user data, confidential datasets, credentials, or sensitive evaluation examples.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Unwanted sexual attention or imagery.
- Doxing or publishing private information.
- Trolling, repeated disruption, or bad-faith participation.
- Sharing secrets, private datasets, or real user conversations without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

If you need to report a conduct issue, contact the maintainers privately. Reports will be handled with discretion and proportionality.
